What our agents keep in RawTree

The shop's data is only half of what lives in RawTree. Every agent and every service we built writes to one table of ours, team_01.scratch. Each kind of row has its own fields, and RawTree needs no schema for them: a new kind of row is just a new insert, queried with the same SQL.
